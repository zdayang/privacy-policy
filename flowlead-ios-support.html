怎样开始？

主页选择声音和时长后点开始，不用先填写目标或注册。工具中可选择倒计时、正计时、番茄钟、呼吸节奏、小憩及晨间提醒。

没有声音、音量不合适？

检查应用内音量、静音、系统音量及蓝牙输出设备。十种环境实录随应用离线提供，播放时不用下载。混音最多三轨，可分别调节音量；保存组合不等于开始播放。

睡前结束时会响吗？

默认不响。定时播放会逐渐降低声音并停止，结束提示音可在设置中单独控制，与专注模式的提示音分开。

提醒没响怎么办？

先检查应用的提醒授权及系统设置。iOS 26 及以后使用系统闹钟能力；旧版本使用本地通知，可能受静音、勿扰或通知设置影响。重要起床需求请同时使用可靠的独立闹钟。此功能不监测睡眠，也不判断浅睡阶段。

记录与旧购买

记录、收藏、混音保存在本机，没有跨设备同步。历史导出会将 Markdown 复制到剪贴板。当前核心功能免费；设置中的既有购买恢复需要联网，并使用原购买 Apple 账户。旧版数据或购买问题请联系支持。

Get started

Choose a sound and duration on the home screen, then press play. No goal form or sign-up. Tools contains countdown, count-up, Pomodoro, breathing rhythms, nap and morning reminders.

Audio and volume

Check in-app volume, mute, system volume and Bluetooth output. Ten environment recordings are included offline. Mix up to three tracks with separate volumes. Saving a mix does not start playback.

Wind-down end chime

It is off by default. Timed sound gradually fades out and stops. Control its end chime separately from focus in Settings.

Reminders

Check permission and system settings. iOS26 and later use system alarms; older versions use local notifications, which can be affected by silent, Focus or notification settings. Use a separate reliable alarm for essential wake-up needs. FlowLead does not monitor sleep or detect sleep stages.

Records and previous purchases

History, favorites and mixes stay on this device; cross-device sync is not available. History export copies Markdown to the clipboard. Current core features are free.
