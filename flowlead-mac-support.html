关闭窗口后如何回来？

点击屏幕右上角菜单栏中的 FlowLead 图标。通过应用菜单或 Command-comma 打开设置；要完全退出，请在菜单中选择退出。

如何使用横向窗口、最大化和全屏？

拖动窗口边缘可自由调整大小；窗口变宽后首页会自动采用横向布局，也可用系统窗口控件最大化或进入全屏。重新打开或退出后再启动会记住窗口大小和位置；屏幕更换时会把窗口收回可见区域。窄窗口仍可使用，不影响 iPhone 的布局。

没有声音或音量不合适？

检查应用内音量、静音和系统输出设备。混音最多有三轨，每轨可单独调节。十段实录声音随应用离线提供，播放不需要下载。图像是场景示意，不保证与声音来自同一地点。

如何选择计时？

在工具中打开专注计时，可选择倒计时、正计时或番茄钟。历史导出会把 Markdown 复制到剪贴板，不直接保存文件。记录、收藏和混音保存在本机，当前没有跨设备同步。

睡前结束时会响吗？

默认不响，睡前定时会逐渐降低背景音后停止。结束提示音在设置中单独控制，与专注模式的设置分开。

小憩或晨间提醒为什么没有响？

检查系统通知权限、勿扰、静音和电脑休眠。Mac 版使用系统通知，不保证唤醒休眠电脑，也不能代替必须响铃的独立闹钟。重要起床需求请同时使用可靠的其他闹钟。

为什么离开应用会暂停？

若启用了“离开专注时暂停”，请检查允许应用列表或关闭该选项。它只在本机判断应用切换，不封锁其他应用。

Reopen the window

Use FlowLead's icon in the top-right menu bar. Open Settings from the app menu or with Command-comma. Choose Quit in the menu to exit completely.

Wide windows, maximize and full screen

Drag a window edge to resize it. The home screen adapts to a horizontal layout when space allows. Use standard Mac window controls to maximize or enter full screen. The app remembers window size and position when reopened or relaunched, and keeps restored windows within the available display. Compact windows remain supported; iPhone layouts are unchanged.

Audio and volume

Check in-app volume, mute and your system output device. Mix up to three recordings with separate layer volumes. All ten recordings are included offline and do not require downloading during playback. Scene artwork is illustrative and does not claim to show the recording location.

Choose a timer

Open the focus timer in Tools to choose countdown, count-up or Pomodoro. History export copies Markdown to the clipboard; it does not directly save a file. History, favorites and mixes remain on this device. Cross-device sync is not available.

Wind-down end chime

It is off by default. Timed wind-down playback gradually fades out and stops. Its end chime is controlled separately from focus mode in Settings.

Nap and morning reminders

Check notification permission, Focus, silent settings and computer sleep. Mac reminders use system notifications and are not guaranteed to wake a sleeping computer. Use another reliable alarm for essential wake-up needs.

Playback pauses when you switch apps

If pause-on-leaving-focus is enabled, check your allowed-app list or turn the option off. App changes are checked locally; other apps are not blocked.

Free features and previous purchases

Current Mac 1.4.1 core features are free. Previous Purchases in Settings provides restoration; a network connection and the purchasing Apple account are required. Contact support for local-data clearing or purchase questions. Never send your Apple password.
