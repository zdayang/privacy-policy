FlowLead iPhone 隐私政策

本页适用于 iOS 1.4.1；该版本的发布状态以 App Store 为准，不代表本文发布时应用已经上线。线上旧版 iOS 1.1.2 的 PostHog 使用统计和 Mac 版说明继续见其他版本政策，不可把本版关闭统计理解为所有旧版本都不联网。

本机数据与统计

专注、休息与呼吸记录、可选标签、收藏、混音、声音偏好和提醒设置保存在本机。iOS 1.4.1 关闭了产品使用统计，不初始化 PostHog、不上传这些行为记录、不启用会话录像。没有账号注册、云同步、睡眠监测或录音功能。用户主动导出历史时，Markdown 会复制到剪贴板；后续系统同步或分享取决于用户设置。

既有购买与第三方服务

当前核心功能免费，无需购买。为识别与恢复既有购买，应用仍初始化 RevenueCat，并使用 Apple 购买服务。它们处理购买历史、收据或交易状态、匿名应用用户标识和服务所需技术信息；状态检查与恢复需要联网。应用不设置姓名、邮箱或电话作为自定义购买属性，也不提供自定义登录账号。付款由 Apple 处理，我们不获取银行卡号或安全码。参见 Apple 与 RevenueCat 的隐私政策。

权限和系统功能

仅在用户启用小憩或晨间提醒时申请相应授权：iOS 26 及以后使用 AlarmKit，旧系统使用通知。可在系统设置撤销。可选的翻转专注仅在本机读取设备加速度判断朝向，不上传传感器样本。后台播放与系统播放控制会向系统提供当前声音标题与播放状态。无需麦克风、摄像头、定位、通讯录、健康数据或 iCloud 访问。没有睡眠录音、阶段检测或医疗功能。

分享、保存与删除

不提供广告追踪，不出售个人数据，不要求广告标识访问。本机记录保留至用户按对应版本的方式清理；系统备份、卸载及自行导出的副本受系统和用户设置影响，不承诺卸载即删除所有副本。购买服务可能按其政策保留安全、交易或法定义务需要的数据。需要清理本机数据，或查询、删除可定位的服务记录，请联系支持；随机标识可能限制具体记录的定位，我们不会要求密码或付款卡资料。

FlowLead iPhone Privacy Policy

This page applies to iOS1.4.1. Availability is determined by the App Store; publication of this policy does not mean that the app version is already live. See the other-version policy for the public iOS1.1.2 release with PostHog analytics and Mac behavior. Disabling analytics in this version does not disable networking in older releases.

Local records and analytics

Focus, break and breathing history, optional labels, favorites, mixes, sound preferences and reminder settings are stored locally. Product usage analytics are disabled in iOS1.4.1: PostHog is not initialized, these behavior records are not uploaded, and session recording is not enabled. There is no sign-up, cloud sync, sleep monitoring or recording. A user-requested history export copies Markdown to the clipboard. Subsequent system synchronization or sharing depends on your settings.

Previous purchases and providers

Current core features are free. RevenueCat remains initialized to recognize and restore previous purchases, using Apple purchase services. These providers process purchase history, receipts or transaction status, anonymous app-user identifiers and technical information needed for the service. Status checks and restoration require a connection. The app does not set names, emails or phone numbers as custom purchase-user attributes or provide a custom sign-in account. Apple processes payment; we do not receive card numbers or security codes. See Apple and RevenueCat.

Permissions and system features

Permission is requested only when you enable nap or morning reminders: AlarmKit on iOS26 and later, notifications on older systems. You can revoke permission in system settings. Optional flip-focus uses accelerometer readings locally to determine orientation; sensor samples are not uploaded. Background audio and system playback controls share the current sound title and playback state with the operating system. No microphone, camera, location, contacts, health data or iCloud access is needed. There is no sleep recording, sleep-stage detection or medical functionality.

Sharing, retention and deletion

We do not provide advertising tracking, sell personal information or request advertising-identifier access. Local records remain until cleared using your version's available method. System backups, uninstall behavior and copies you export depend on system and user settings; uninstalling is not a promise that every copy is deleted. Purchase providers may retain data required for security, transactions or legal duties under their policies. Contact support for local-data clearing or requests concerning identifiable service records. Random identifiers may limit locating specific records. We never need passwords or payment-card details.
