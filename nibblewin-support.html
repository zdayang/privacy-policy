Common questions

Where is my data stored?
Your check-ins, garden, reminders, and preferences are stored on this iPhone. There is no account or cloud backup.

How do I stop anonymous analytics?
Open Settings in NibbleWin, choose Privacy Policy, and turn off “Share anonymous app usage.”

How do I delete my data?
Open Settings and choose “Delete all local data.” NibbleWin requires several confirmations because deletion cannot be undone.

Is NibbleWin medical treatment?
No. NibbleWin supports gentle habit reflection and is not medical advice, nutrition treatment, or eating-disorder treatment.
