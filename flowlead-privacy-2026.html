FlowLead 隐私政策

本政策说明 FlowLead 的本机数据及第三方服务。本次更新涵盖 Mac 1.4.1，以及目前公开发行的 iOS 1.1.2。不同版本的数据行为不同，不能把 Mac 版关闭统计理解为所有已发行版本都不联网。

1. 保存在设备上的信息

Mac 1.4.1 的专注与休息记录、可选标签、收藏、混音组合、声音偏好、提醒设置及窗口大小和位置保存在本机；不上传这些记录用于产品行为分析。此版无账号注册、云同步、睡眠监测或录音功能。用户主动导出历史时，应用将 Markdown 文本复制到剪贴板，由用户决定后续如何使用。剪贴板的后续同步或共享取决于用户的系统设置。

2. 当前线上 iOS 版本的匿名使用统计

iOS 1.1.2 使用 PostHog 记录应用启动、专注开始与结束、产出创建和结果标记等事件，用来了解产品使用情况。事件可能包含随机安装标识、应用版本、系统与设备信息、语言、是否为 Pro，以及来源或活动标记。可识别一次安装的随机标识不等于姓名，但也不是完全不可关联的数据。目标正文、产出正文、笔记等自由文本不会作为这些事件的属性上传；未启用会话录像。

Mac 1.4.1 的产品使用统计已关闭。较旧 iOS 版本的统计控制如有差异，以该版本实际设置为准；可联系支持了解版本适用情况。PostHog 的处理说明见 PostHog 隐私政策。

3. Apple 与旧版购买记录

Mac 1.4.1 当前核心功能免费，不要求购买。为识别和恢复既有购买，仍使用 RevenueCat 与 Apple 购买服务；其他已发行版本也可能使用这些服务。它们处理购买历史、收据或交易状态、匿名应用用户标识和服务所需的技术信息。状态检查和恢复购买需要网络。应用没有把姓名、邮箱或电话号码作为自定义购买用户属性设置，也不向 RevenueCat 提供自定义登录账号。

付款由 Apple 处理，我们不获取银行卡号、安全码等付款卡资料。Apple 与 RevenueCat 的数据处理请参阅 Apple 隐私政策和 RevenueCat 隐私政策。离线声音播放不等于购买服务也无需联网。

4. 权限与系统集成

Mac 版仅在开启小憩或晨间提醒时请求通知权限。勿扰、静音和电脑休眠可能影响提醒，不能依赖它作为必须响铃的唯一闹钟。可选的“离开专注时暂停”在本机观察前台应用切换及用户允许的应用列表，不把该列表上传作分析，也不封锁其他应用。

Mac 1.4.1 不需要麦克风、摄像头、定位、通讯录或 iCloud 访问来提供这些功能。沙盒中的用户选定文件权限为兼容既有导出流程保留；当前历史导出使用剪贴板，不扫描文件夹，也不获得任意文件访问。系统服务与用户主动启用的系统功能按其自身设置运行。

5. 分享与用途

我们不以广告追踪为目的组合其他公司的用户数据，不出售用户个人数据，不要求广告标识权限。上述购买与统计供应商仅按相应服务处理信息；用户主动发给支持的邮件由所用邮件服务处理。用户自行复制、导出或分享的内容由用户决定去向。

6. 保存与删除

本机记录会在设备上保留，直到用户按相应版本提供的方式清理；卸载 Mac 应用不一定清除所有容器数据或用户副本。需要本机数据清理步骤，或希望查询、删除购买/分析服务中可定位的数据，可联系下方支持邮箱。随机标识可能限制我们把远端记录定位到具体个人；我们会说明所需的最少信息，不要求密码或付款卡资料。供应商为安全、交易、法定义务保留的信息按其政策处理；本政策不虚构统一保存期限。

7. 联系与更新

隐私与支持邮箱：wpqingwa123@gmail.com。请注明 FlowLead 版本、平台和请求类型，不要发送密码或付款卡资料。数据行为改变时会更新本政策及适用的商店申报。

FlowLead Privacy Policy

This policy explains local information and third-party services in FlowLead. This update covers Mac 1.4.1 and the currently public iOS 1.1.2 release. Behavior differs by version: disabling analytics on Mac does not mean that every released version has no network activity.

1. Information on your device

Mac 1.4.1 stores focus and break history, optional labels, favorites, mixes, sound preferences, reminder settings, and window size and position locally. It does not upload those records for product usage analytics. This version has no account sign-up, cloud sync, sleep monitoring or recording. A user-requested history export copies Markdown text to the clipboard; you decide how to use it. Any subsequent clipboard synchronization or sharing depends on your system settings.

2. Analytics in the current public iOS release

iOS 1.1.2 uses PostHog to record events such as app opens, focus starts and completions, output creation and result marking. Events may contain a random installation identifier, app version, system and device information, language, Pro status, and source or campaign markers. A random identifier can distinguish an installation; it is not a name, but is not necessarily unrelatable information. Goal text, output text, notes and other free-form content are not included as event properties. Session recording is not enabled.

Product usage analytics are disabled in Mac 1.4.1. Controls in older iOS versions may differ; contact support for version-specific information. See PostHog's privacy policy.

3. Apple and previous purchases

Current Mac 1.4.1 core features are free and require no purchase. RevenueCat and Apple purchase services remain available to recognize and restore existing purchases; other released versions may also use them. They process purchase history, receipt or transaction status, anonymous app user identifiers and technical information needed for the service. Purchase-status checks and restoration require a network connection. The app does not set a name, email or phone number as a custom purchase-user attribute or provide a custom sign-in account to RevenueCat.

Apple processes payments. We do not receive payment-card numbers or security codes. See Apple's privacy policy and RevenueCat's privacy policy. Offline audio playback does not make the purchase service offline.

4. Permissions and system integration

The Mac app requests notification permission only when you enable nap or morning reminders. Focus, silent settings and computer sleep may affect delivery; do not rely on this app as your only essential wake-up alarm. The optional pause-on-leaving-focus feature observes foreground app changes and your allowed-app list locally. It does not upload that list for analytics or block other apps.

These Mac 1.4.1 features do not require microphone, camera, location, contacts or iCloud access. A user-selected-file sandbox entitlement is retained for compatibility with previous export workflows. Current history export uses the clipboard; the app does not scan folders or obtain unrestricted file access. System services and features you choose to enable operate under their own settings.

5. Sharing and purposes

We do not combine data with other companies' user data for advertising tracking, sell personal information, or request advertising-identifier access. The purchase and analytics providers above process information for their respective services. Support emails are processed by the email service used. You control any content you copy, export or share yourself.

6. Retention and deletion

Local records remain until cleared using the method available in your version. Uninstalling a Mac app may not remove every container file or copy you have made. Contact support for local-data clearing instructions, or to request access to or deletion of identifiable purchase/analytics records. Random identifiers may limit our ability to locate records for a specific person. We will explain the minimum information needed and will not ask for passwords or payment-card details. Providers retain information required for security, transactions or legal duties under their policies; this policy does not promise a single invented retention period.

7. Contact and updates

Privacy and support: wpqingwa123@gmail.com. Include your app version, platform and request type, not passwords or card details. We will update this policy and applicable store declarations when data practices change.
